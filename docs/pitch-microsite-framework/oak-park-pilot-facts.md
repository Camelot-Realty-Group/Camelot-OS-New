## What the Boards told Camelot is missing

1. A property manager physically on-site weekly, checking in with the superintendent/staff and visually inspecting grounds/common areas/facilities
2. Monthly financial and management reports that arrive consistently on a fixed date
3. Active arrears follow-up — aging report, documented collection actions, escalation to counsel under Board policy
4. A facilities professional reviewing mechanical systems, training staff, building SOPs/checklists
5. Real cost benchmarking against a portfolio, vendor rebids where there's a credible savings case
6. Better communication between managing agent, Boards, superintendent, residents, vendors
7. A transition team that captures the full ledger, owner data, arrears, contracts, notices, physical files, and institutional knowledge — not just a name change on the invoice

## Financial proposal (working basis)

- **Recommended fee**: $85/unit/month → $70/unit to the applicable condominium entity + $15/unit (across all 213 units) to the UOA
- At the working 213-unit count: Condo I $8,470/mo ($70 × 121), Condo II $6,440/mo ($70 × 92), UOA $3,195/mo ($15 × 213), **total $18,105/month / $217,260/year**
- Range considered: $75/unit floor ($15,975/mo, $191,700/yr) to $100/unit ceiling ($21,300/mo, $255,600/yr)
- 3% annual escalator after Year 1; ancillary rates fixed for Year 1, no change thereafter without written Board approval
- One regular Board meeting/month included (a joint/consecutive same-evening session across all three entities counts as one); each additional separate Board meeting is $150 (three separate meetings in a month = $300 in additional fees)
- One annual owner meeting per entity included; extraordinary special owner meetings $150 each unless separately scoped

## 60–90 day transition plan

- **Days 0–30**: confirm three legal entities, unit ownership records, budgets, bank accounts, contracts, employees, insurance, vendors, arrears, litigation, compliance; obtain full GL/trial balance/reconciliations/owner balances per entity; establish payment cutoff date; collect governing documents, board minutes, contracts, employee/insurance/legal files, physical keys/access; log an Incomplete Records / Deficiency Log for anything missing
- **Days 31–60**: weekly site rhythm begins; staff/SOP training; resident onboarding (welcome communication, portal registration, new payment instructions); Camelot Welcome Event / wine-and-cheese meet-and-greet with PM, Valerie, Tim, and David where practical; facilities/mechanical review; budget and vendor benchmark review
- **Days 61–90**: first reconciled monthly reporting cycle closes; arrears action plan; contract rebids; compliance/violation status verified; Oak Park 60-Day Property & Operations Brief (visual/maintenance/mechanical/grounds/staffing/compliance observations, cost-reduction opportunities, SOP recommendations, priority capital items) delivered to the Boards
